Structured Intelligence Lab

We study reliable and efficient learning methods for structured visual data, with a focus on 3D vision, medical imaging, and trustworthy machine learning.

College of AI Convergence, Korea Aerospace University

Research Topics

  1. 1

    3D Representation and Reconstruction

    Neural representation, surface reconstruction, 3D generation, and neural rendering.

  2. 2

    Medical Image Analysis

    Deformable registration, multimodal alignment, and missing-data imputation.

  3. 3

    Trustworthy Machine Learning

    Robustness, uncertainty, bias mitigation, and learning under distribution shift.
